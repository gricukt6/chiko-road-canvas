Chiko Road Canvas is a farm-map logic puzzle where you paint road tiles from number clues until every nest on the canvas is linked. A round is won when the whole road is painted, and it ends if you make a third mistake or run out of moves; marking a cell you know is empty spends a move but never counts as a mistake, and a hint reveals one correct tile at the cost of two moves. Success is skill, not luck: each of the twelve canvases is a fixed, solvable layout, from Easy 5×5 boards through Normal 7×7 to Hard 8×8. Rewards stay in the game — one to three stars for a clean run with moves left, an accuracy score, and the next canvas unlocking — and your best accuracy is kept on the menu. There are no cash prizes and nothing to cash out, and the campaign has no in-app purchases.
